Privacy Policy for Swipe the Flow

Swipe the Flow is an interactive fluid simulation app for iPhone and iPad. This page explains how the app handles privacy, advertising, and device permissions.

No account required. Photos access is optional. Ads are provided through Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Effective Date

May 8, 2026

Overview

Swipe the Flow does not require account creation or user login.

The app itself does not provide developer-operated account, messaging, or cloud storage features. However, the app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to display ads, and Google may process certain data for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and ad performance measurement.

Advertising

Swipe the Flow uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

Depending on your device settings, region, and permissions, Google may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, ad impressions, and ad clicks to provide, improve, personalize, and measure ads.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

Photos Permission

Swipe the Flow may request access to your Photos library only when you choose to save a screenshot or recorded video. This access is used solely to save content you create in the app to your device's Photos library.

Developer Data Handling

Swipe the Flow does not create user accounts and does not directly collect or store personal data on developer-operated servers through its own app features.

Third-Party Services

Swipe the Flow uses Google Mobile Ads SDK as a third-party service provider for advertising.

Children's Privacy

Swipe the Flow is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated features. Third-party advertising services may process data as described above.
