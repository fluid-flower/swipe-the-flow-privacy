Privacy Policy for Swipe the Flow

Swipe the Flow is an interactive fluid simulation app for iPhone and iPad. This page explains how the app handles privacy and device permissions.

No data collected. No account required.

Effective Date

May 8, 2026

Overview

Swipe the Flow does not collect, store, or share personal data.

Photos Permission

Swipe the Flow may request access to your Photos library only when you choose to save a screenshot or recorded video. This access is used solely to save content you create in the app to your device's Photos library.

Data Collection

Swipe the Flow does not collect personal information, does not create user accounts, and does not transmit your data to external servers.

Third-Party Sharing

Swipe the Flow does not share personal data with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Swipe the Flow does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
